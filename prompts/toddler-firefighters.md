# Toddler Firefighters

A high-energy cinematic Disney-Pixar style animated YouTube Shorts adventure
about adorable toddler firefighters inside a busy fantasy city. The video
begins at sunrise inside a colorful toy-like fire station in the middle of a
giant animated city filled with tall buildings, flying birds, busy streets,
and glowing signs. Make the pacing fast — a new scene every 2 seconds for
YouTube Shorts retention. With visually rich and exciting action scenes.

## Camera directions

- Dramatic fire alarm close-up
- Fast tracking shots beside moving fire truck
- Cinematic aerial shots of city
- Emotional close-ups
- Dynamic firefighting shots
- Fire truck over city at sunset

## Avoid

During firefighting: violence, human injuries, horror fire, scary injuries,
dark atmosphere, gore, creepy visuals, blurry animation, distorted hands,
realistic destruction, text, fire, creepy expressions.
